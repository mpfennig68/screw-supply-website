// FAQ

Common questions

How do I choose the matching spanner bit?
The tool size and pin spacing must match the holes in the fastener head. Send the part number or a photo and dimensions if the correct tool is uncertain.
Are spanner screws removable?
Yes, with a compatible matching tool. They discourage casual tampering but are not removal-proof.
What is an undercut head?
An undercut flat or oval head has a reduced head depth for its configuration. Keep undercut part numbers distinct from standard heads and confirm dimensions before substituting.
Which screw forms are listed?
The tables separate machine screws and sheet-metal screws. Sizes are in inches; thread pitch appears in machine-screw sizes.
Can you review custom sizes or materials?
Yes. Send a drawing or sample information with the thread, length, head style, material, finish, quantity, and any documentation requirements. We offer custom-manufactured fasteners with competitive minimum order quantities.
