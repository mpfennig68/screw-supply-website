Tri-Groove Security Sockets

Tri-Groove security sockets fit over the rounded heads of compatible screws or nuts and engage their three external grooves for installation and authorized removal. Use the chart below to identify the catalog-listed socket for your fastener.

// FAQ

Common questions

How do I choose the correct Tri-Groove socket?
Use the size chart, then confirm the socket matches your fastener series and head or nut profile. Thread size alone does not guarantee fit.
What clearance is needed for installation?
Allow enough space around and above the fastener for the socket to seat fully. Fully engage the socket before applying torque, and retain it for future service.
Will the socket fit my existing ratchet or handle?
Confirm the connection for the specific socket before ordering. Send your handle details if you need help matching it.
Can you identify an unknown fastener or replacement socket?
Yes. Send a clear photo, dimensions, or sample information so we can help identify the matching socket.
What information should I send for a quote?
Include the socket part number if known, fastener type and size, quantity, and whether you need a handle. Mention any access restrictions and attach a photo or drawing if helpful.
