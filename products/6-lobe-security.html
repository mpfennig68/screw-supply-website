// FAQ

Common questions

Can a standard solid-tip six-lobe bit turn these screws?
The center pin prevents the intended engagement with a solid-tip bit. Use a matching pin-in security bit or key of the drive size listed for the part.
Does screw diameter determine the drive size?
No. Head style and configuration can change the required drive size. Read the part-specific drive column, including undercut-head entries.
Which thread form should I choose?
Cap screws fit a compatible tapped hole or nut. Sheet-metal, self-drilling, and masonry screws have different receiving-material requirements; identify the substrate and installation before ordering.
What do the size and material tables represent?
They reproduce listed catalog configurations, not a live stock feed. Sizes are in inches, with material and finish separated by group. Ask us about requirements outside these listings.
Can you review custom sizes or materials?
Yes. Send a drawing or sample information with the thread, length, head style, material, finish, quantity, and any documentation requirements. We offer custom-manufactured fasteners with competitive minimum order quantities.
