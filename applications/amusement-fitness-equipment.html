// WHY IT MATTERS

Amusement rides, playground structures, and fitness equipment carry real consequences if a fastener works loose or is removed by an unauthorized hand. Pin-in hex and 6-lobe drives let maintenance crews service assemblies on schedule while keeping the traveling public from ever touching structural hardware.
