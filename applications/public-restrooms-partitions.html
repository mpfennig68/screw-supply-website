// WHY IT MATTERS

Public restrooms see constant unsupervised traffic, which makes them one of the most common targets for fastener tampering and petty theft. Partition hinges, grab bars, dispensers, and mirror mounts all need to stay fixed in place without inviting removal. A standard Phillips or slotted screw gives anyone with a pocket screwdriver an easy way in — a pin-in or two-hole security drive does not.
