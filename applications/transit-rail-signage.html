// WHY IT MATTERS

Transit systems mount hardware in exposed, unmonitored locations — platform signage, shelter panels, rail car interiors — where fasteners are both weathered and within public reach around the clock. Security drives keep panels and signage from being pried loose or unbolted for scrap or vandalism, while corrosion-resistant materials hold up outdoors over years of service.
