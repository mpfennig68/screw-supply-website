// WHY IT MATTERS

Electrical panels, junction boxes, and equipment enclosures carry real liability if opened by the wrong person — arc flash risk, warranty voids, tampering with metering or controls. A pin-in security drive lets your own service techs carry the matching bit while keeping the enclosure closed to everyone else.
